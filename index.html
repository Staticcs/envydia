Who she is

These stay the same in every picture. If an image gets them right, it is her.

Hair
Glossy black bob, cut blunt at the jaw, with straight bangs.
Streak
One green streak at the front, on her right. It reads on the left of the picture. #76B900
Mic
Black headset mic. The boom comes from her right and ends in a foam tip beside her mouth.
Hoops
Large, thin silver hoop earrings.
Face
Pale skin, freckles across the nose and cheeks, blue-grey eyes, dark liner. Calm and direct. She rarely smiles.
Jacket
Fitted black jacket with a high collar and a green zip.
Legs
Black cargo trousers cuffed at the ankle, black ankle boots.
Age
A grown woman. Never write girl or young in a prompt.
Colour
Black, plus whatever the setting brings. Green is the only accent she carries.
Medium
Photographic. Never illustrated or cel-shaded.

Make her

One prompt, one reference image and three checks.

Prompt

Start with this, add a line for the setting, and attach the face sheet as your reference image.

Envydia, a grown woman, glossy black blunt bob with straight bangs, one green streak (#76B900) at the front on her right side, black headset mic with the boom on her right, large thin silver hoop earrings, pale skin with freckles, blue-grey eyes, dark eyeliner, calm direct gaze, fitted black high-collar jacket with a green zip, black cargo trousers, black ankle boots, photographic, green as the only accent colour

Download face sheet

Check

  1. The green streak is on her right, which is the left side of the picture.
  2. The mic boom is on the same side as the streak.
  3. The zip is green and the hoops are silver.

Models like to mirror her. If the streak lands on the wrong side, flip the image or throw it out.

Rules

She is open. Make pictures, films, songs and merch with her, sell them, and you do not need to ask.

  • She is a grown woman. Keep her one.
  • No sexual content.
  • Do not present your work as official, or as connected to any company.

Credit is welcome and not required.
