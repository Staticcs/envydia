# Envydia

and attach /img/face-sheet.jpg from the site this file came from as the reference image.

## Identity prompt
Envydia, a grown woman, glossy black blunt bob with straight bangs, one green streak (#76B900) at the front on her right side, black headset mic with the boom on her right, large thin silver hoop earrings, pale skin with freckles, blue-grey eyes, dark eyeliner, calm direct gaze, fitted black high-collar jacket with a green zip, black cargo trousers, black ankle boots, photographic, green as the only accent colour

## Canon
- Hair: Glossy black bob, cut blunt at the jaw, with straight bangs.
- Streak: One green streak at the front, on her right. It reads on the left of the picture.
- Mic: Black headset mic. The boom comes from her right and ends in a foam tip beside her mouth.
- Hoops: Large, thin silver hoop earrings.
- Face: Pale skin, freckles across the nose and cheeks, blue-grey eyes, dark liner. Calm and direct. She rarely smiles.
- Jacket: Fitted black jacket with a high collar and a green zip.
- Legs: Black cargo trousers cuffed at the ankle, black ankle boots.
- Age: A grown woman. Never write girl or young in a prompt.
- Colour: Black, plus whatever the setting brings. Green is the only accent she carries.
- Medium: Photographic. Never illustrated or cel-shaded.

## Checks before you return a picture
1. The green streak is on her right, which is the left side of the picture.
2. The mic boom is on the same side as the streak.
3. The zip is green and the hoops are silver.

Models often mirror her. If the streak is on the wrong side, flip the image or discard it.
